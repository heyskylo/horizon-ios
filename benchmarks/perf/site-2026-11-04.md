# Horizon Nightly 2026-11-04 vs vanilla 26.3 on the Mac mini and the desktop (2026-11-04)

> **The Mac mini reports (`*-macmini-rd*.json`) are offscreen, not comparable.** Horizon rendered offscreen (no presenting)
> while vanilla drew into a window, so the Mac rows below are not a Horizon-vs-vanilla comparison, and the website does not
> publish them: it keeps the Mac mini's 2026-10-22 runs. They are kept here only as a record of that day's offscreen run.

The desktop runs are the ones behind playhorizon.dev/benchmarks as of 2026-11-04 (horizon-rust/horizon-site#58): `tools/perf_bench.py`
reports, render distance 12 and 32, three games each, every game from a fresh world (seed 1234), 1280x720, VSync off. Files:
`{horizon,vanilla}-macmini-rd{12,32}.json` and `{horizon,vanilla}-desktop-rd{12,32}.json` next to this page.

## Builds

- **Horizon:** the Nightly 2026-11-04 release bundle (67dad0d), as the launcher installs it: macOS arm64 on the Mac,
  Linux x86_64 on the desktop. Its resources come from the bundle's own `horizon-prepare`. perf_bench ran from main
  a55b9a2e52 and wrote that checkout's crate version and commit; `app.version` and `app.commit` were set to the bundle's by hand.
- **Vanilla:** the 26.3 client jar with Fabric Loader and the driver mod, Java 25, the official launcher's JVM flags.

## Machines and conditions

- **Mac mini, Apple M4 Pro (14 cores, 20-core GPU), macOS: offscreen, not comparable.** The session was locked, so Horizon ran `--offscreen`: a locked
  session gives a window no frames, and windowed Horizon measured 0 FPS. Vanilla drew into its window. Offscreen skips
  presenting, which favours Horizon, so its averages aren't held at the 144 Hz display as they were on 2026-10-22. Each game
  started at a 1-minute load of 17-18 (the run waited for < 20); during the runs the load rose to 30-50.
- **Desktop, RTX 4080 + i7-13700KF, NixOS, NVIDIA 595.** Both games drew into a headless sway rendering on the RTX 4080 (never
  on its screen), both through Wayland on Vulkan. Vanilla went through SDL's Wayland driver: through Xwayland each of its
  frames took about 2 s on this day. The machine also runs CI and agents' builds: each game started at a load of 28-30 on
  24 threads (the run waited for < 30) and the load rose to 40-70 during the runs. Vanilla at RD 32 crashed twice under that
  load (`GPU timeout attempting to acquire next frame`) before a third series finished. Start-up times are inflated by the
  load and by a cold disk on each series' first start (start → world up to 130-235 s).
- **Order:** RD 12 ran Horizon then vanilla; RD 32 ran vanilla then Horizon.
