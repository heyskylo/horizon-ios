# Loaded furnace cooking totals

`AbstractFurnaceBlockEntity.loadAdditional` loads a missing total as zero.
`serverTick` increments the loaded spent value, compares it with the stored total,
and only after completion replaces the total with the recipe duration. Changing
an input separately updates its duration through `setItem`. H119 repairs only
the unconditional early reset in `FurnaceCooking::tick`.

The actorless real Game/Java fixture loads five lit furnaces with remaining/total
burn20, raw_iron3 and no fuel. Cases are missing/zero/five/200 total at spent0,
and five at spent1. It observes cumulative0/1/4/5/6 actual ticks. Every observation
has positive loaded, floor, furnace id/coordinates and lit-state guards, complete
slot values, spent/total, remaining/total burn and exact relative clock deltas.

Fresh pinned Java26.3 admits first completion at1 for missing/zero, at5 for
five/spent0, and at4 for five/spent1. The 200 control does not complete. Input and
output counts and the post-completion200 duration are checked independently.
