dxcompiler.dll in this folder is the DirectX Shader Compiler (DXC) v1.9.2609 by Microsoft Corporation,
unmodified, from https://github.com/microsoft/DirectXShaderCompiler/releases/tag/v1.9.2609
(dxc_2026_09_29.zip, bin/x64/dxcompiler.dll). Horizon loads it only on integrated GPUs with DirectX 12,
to compile its shaders.

It is not part of Horizon and not covered by Horizon's MIT licence. It is distributed under Microsoft's
licence terms in DXC-LICENSE-MS.txt (its open-source part also under DXC-LICENSE-LLVM.txt), and using
it means accepting those terms. If you pass this folder on, keep these three files with the DLL.

If you don't accept them, delete dxcompiler.dll: Horizon then compiles its shaders with FXC, part of
Windows, and runs as before.
